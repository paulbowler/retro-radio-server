# Retro Radio logo

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Used as app branding and fallback artwork; radios receive a 128 × 128 JPEG version.

## Prompt

Create a square logo for RETRO RADIO, a revived internet radio service, designed to look excellent on a small 128x128 pixel radio screen. Opaque very dark purple background #0c0718. Bold central simplified vintage portable radio icon with thick neon cyan #45f4e5 outline, hot pink #ff4ca8 accents, a simple speaker circle and tuning bar. Large bold readable stacked words RETRO RADIO, with retro 1980s geometric lettering, cyan and pink. Restrained neon glow, crisp clean shapes, high contrast, balanced composition, generous safe margins. No tiny details, no fine grid, no mockup, no surrounding objects, no watermark. The logo fills most of the square and is a finished standalone brand artwork.
